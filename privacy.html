Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Welcome to QuickPDF. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and online PDF tools.

1. Information We Collect

QuickPDF does not require you to create an account to use the basic PDF tools.

Information you enter into the PDF builder, such as text used to create a document, is processed by the browser for the applicable client-side tools.

2. Uploaded Files

The basic text and image PDF creation features process files directly in your browser and do not require uploading those files to our own server.

You should still avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into any online service unless you are comfortable doing so.

3. Cookies

QuickPDF may use cookies or similar technologies if third-party services such as analytics or advertising services are added to the website in the future.

4. Advertising

QuickPDF may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use technologies such as cookies to provide and measure advertisements, subject to their own policies and applicable requirements.

5. Third-Party Services

QuickPDF may use third-party libraries or services to provide website functionality. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

QuickPDF is intended to provide general-purpose online PDF tools. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to provide a safe website experience. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, features, or services change. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the QuickPDF website.
